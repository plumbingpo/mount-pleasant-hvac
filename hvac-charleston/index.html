Complete Heating & Cooling Services for Charleston Properties

Our team works on every one of those systems. Our technicians have years in the field, and we've learned how the weather here, the housing stock, and code requirements affect the way equipment runs over time.

Quick Heating and Cooling Help in Charleston

Heating and cooling failures rarely wait for a convenient day, which is why we offer fast, flexible scheduling for most situations. Our typical arrival window in the area is usually within a day for urgent calls, and a dead AC in a heat wave or a furnace out on a freezing night is the kind of call we move to the front of the line.

The urgent calls we run tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's an AC blowing warm air, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil frozen solid. On the heating side, it's a furnace that lights and then shuts down. Year-round, it's breakers that trip whenever the system starts, plugged condensate drains backing water into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have quit. Since our trucks carry capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, most of these are wrapped up on the first trip, with the price agreed before any work begins.

Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can overflow the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can prevent a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Charleston

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
